cropmark Your photo never leaves this tab.

Crop a photo and mark it up

Drop a JPEG or PNG here, paste one, or press Open.

The photo is opened, edited and saved inside this tab. It is not uploaded anywhere.

A saved file keeps the colour profile and is turned upright. Other EXIF data, including the GPS location, is removed.
